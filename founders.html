Why nilabay?

Born on the water.
Built for more life.

The idea for nilabay began while paddleboarding on Little Traverse Bay in Northern Michigan, watching sunlight shimmer across the ripples.

As an early adopter of AI, I started wondering: what if technology could give people more time for the things that matter? Not just help businesses work faster, but free people to create, explore, build, and pursue what they love.

The name brings together nīla, a Sanskrit word associated with deep blue and sapphire, and Little Traverse Bay, where the idea began.

Today, we bring thoughtful engineering, practical technology, and AI where it makes sense to help people build better businesses and make more room for life.

Because the point of better technology isn't more technology. It's more life.
