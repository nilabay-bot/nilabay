Born on the water.
Built for more life.

The idea for nilabay began while paddleboarding on Little Traverse Bay in Northern Michigan, watching sunlight shimmer across the ripples.

A question followed: what if better technology could give people more time to create, explore, and do what they love?

The name brings together nīla, associated with deep blue and sapphire, and Little Traverse Bay, where the idea began.

Better systems.
More room for life.

That’s what we’re building toward.
