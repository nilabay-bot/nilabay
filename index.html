Employee onboarding & offboarding

What if hiring someone didn't create a week of extra work?

The challenge: A new employee needs accounts, equipment, approvals, and access. Each team has a piece of the process.

The approach: Coordinate steps from one approved request, keep people informed, and track what's complete. The same discipline applies when employees change roles or leave.

Clear ownership. Fewer missed handoffs.
Device & employee records

What if you always knew who had what?

The challenge: Company device records and employee assignments can drift apart.

The approach: Compare records across systems, flag disagreements, and let an authorized person review changes before they happen.

Better records. Less guesswork. Human oversight.
