The kind of problems

A few concrete examples drawn from the kinds of systems and processes our founders have worked with. These are illustrative approaches, not NILABAY client case studies or claims of completed customer projects.

People & access

Someone joins, changes roles, or leaves. Everything stays in step.

The headache: HR, managers, and IT each have part of the story. Accounts, permissions, and equipment can fall through the cracks.

What we could build: One approved request sets off a clear sequence: prepare the right access, notify the right people, track what's done, and flag anything that needs a human decision. When someone leaves, the same process helps make sure access is reviewed and removed.

One request. Clear ownership. Fewer missed handoffs.
Devices & accountability

Know who has each company computer—and whether the records agree.

The headache: Device records, employee accounts, and actual usage don't always match. Someone has to compare them by hand.

What we could build: Compare device enrollment, assigned ownership, and sign-in information; surface mismatches; and send a review list to the right person before records are changed. Keep a trail of what was approved.

Less guesswork. Cleaner records. Human approval where it matters.
Connected operations

Stop re-entering the same information in five places.

The headache: A sale becomes an email, then a spreadsheet, then an invoice, then another status update.

What we could build: Connect the steps so the right information moves between existing systems, exceptions go to a person, and the owner sees what's happening without requesting another report.

Less repetition. More visibility. Room to grow.

Every business has different systems, policies, and approval needs. We assess what can be connected safely before recommending a solution.
